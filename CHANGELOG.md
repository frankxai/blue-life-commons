### Added

- A public Next.js experience for the commons, including the encyclopedia,
  regions, missions, research, catalog, Guardian, impact, governance, privacy,
  contribution, support, services, and ecosystem surfaces.
- A source-linked Deep Time collection covering 13 marine reptiles, with
  reconstruction disclosures, scale comparisons, imagery, and video controls.
- Approved-media records and rendering, media-intelligence views, Vercel Blob
  storage support, licensed source proof, and repository media-debt controls.
- A production Blue Whale record with gallery, hero media, and educational
  detail.
- About and terms routes, public-truth regression tests, and a reusable
  web-excellence gate for future visual work.

### Changed

- Expanded the public encyclopedia from the founding release's 31 species pages
  to 44 current species records while retaining visible citations and review
  state.
- Reworked the repository from a knowledge-corpus foundation into the public
  Blue Life Commons product without changing the commons-first licensing model.
- Strengthened media storage, attribution, rights metadata, and cost/ROI
  documentation for larger collections.

### Security and trust

- Quarantined unreviewed mission instructions and relationship claims from
  public trust surfaces.
- Added built-output, public-link, privacy, contribution, review-state, and
  media-policy gates.
- Bound media-policy approval to an exact pull-request head and execute the
  guard from the trusted base branch.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-06-16

### Added

- The founding open knowledgebase with 31 species pages, eight region
  briefings, four welfare assessments, six dataset cards, sanctuary/rehab and
  stranding templates, and 13 audience guides.
- Schema validation and integrity linting for citations, ethics metadata, and
  review state.
- CC BY 4.0 content licensing and MIT licensing for code.
