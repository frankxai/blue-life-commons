# Blue Life Commons editorial language

## Reader and decision

Write for coastal communities, scientists, funders, policymakers, citizens, and regenerative project builders. The reader should see a credible stewardship model and a practical way to participate.

## Voice

- Clear, humane, ecological, and place-based.
- Name places, species, measurements, and responsible parties.
- Explain governance and shared ownership plainly.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, and proposal.
- End with an action available to the affected community.

## Avoid

- Greenwashing and doom spectacle.
- Generic sustainability language.
- Savior narratives.
- Nature used as branding scenery.
- Global claims without local evidence.

## Examples

“A healthy coast is shared infrastructure. Fishers, swimmers, researchers, and children depend on the same water.”

“Measure the water, publish the result, and let the people living beside it shape the response.”
